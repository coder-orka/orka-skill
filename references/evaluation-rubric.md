# Evaluation rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 5. Pass only when the total is at least 27/30 and no dimension is below 4.

| Dimension | 0-2 | 3 | 4-5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subject fidelity | generic cow or major identity/species loss | source is recognizable but face, markings, pose, or crop drift | same subject, identity/species, colors, pose, action, props, and crop remain clear |
| Bovine integration | no cow cue, pasted or threatening horns, any extra native/hood/decorative ears, missing bell on a visible chest, or a floating/duplicate bell | horns visible but ear replacement or bell placement is incomplete or awkward | exactly one integrated cow-ear pair occupies the original visible-ear anchors across the whole subject and hood; exactly one small rounded horn pair integrates naturally; hidden ears are not invented; exactly one small attached bell hangs at the visible upper chest or is correctly omitted when that area is hidden; optional spots, muzzle, or hooves remain restrained |
| Cuteness and expression | creepy, hostile, stiff, grotesque, or uncanny | neutral and readable but not especially warm | friendly expressive eyes, soft shapes, gentle expression, appealing proportions |
| Materials and form | only the hood/accessories are plush while the animal remains photographic, or yarn, knit, crochet, woven loops, dirty broken texture, excessive seams, or incoherent anatomy | the full animal is mostly plush but pile, padding, seam placement, or material consistency needs refinement | the entire visible animal subject and cow hood form one coherent rounded toy-like 3D treatment with short dense plush, soft padded volume, a few plausible doll seams, readable identity features, and compatible horn material |
| Scene and composition | source environment or framing lost | background recognizable with noticeable drift | original layout, camera distance, crop, props, lighting direction, and subject placement are preserved |
| Overall result | generic mascot, full cow replacement, or unrelated style | partial match with one dominant weakness | clearly reads as the original subject in a cohesive, cute cow-themed transformation |

Record scores in this order: `fidelity / bovine / cute / material / scene / overall = total`.

Do not inflate scores merely because the result is cute. The target requires both strong source preservation and clearly integrated bovine elements.

Automatic fail if the original subject is no longer recognizable, the species or human identity has been replaced by a generic cow, an animal's visible face or body remains photographic/ordinary fur while only its hood or accessories are plush, the required horn pair is absent or duplicated, horns obscure the face, the camera crop changes enough to alter the source composition, clearly visible native ears remain instead of being replaced, more than one ear pair appears anywhere on the subject or hood, a hood adds separate side or decorative ears, cow ears are invented for source ears that are hidden or cropped, a clearly visible upper chest lacks its bell, more than one bell appears, the bell floats without a physical attachment, the crop is changed only to reveal the bell, or the default plush mode visibly uses yarn, knitting, crochet, or woven loops.
